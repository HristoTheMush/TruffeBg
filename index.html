Our Range

Our Truffle Varieties

Black Winter Truffle

Prized for its intense aroma and deep, earthy flavor, the Black Winter Truffle is harvested between December and March. A true delicacy for the winter table.

Black Summer Truffle

Milder and more delicate than its winter cousin, the Black Summer Truffle offers a subtle, nutty aroma, perfect for warm-weather dishes.

White Alba Truffle

Native to the hills of Piedmont, the White Alba Truffle is celebrated as the most precious of all, prized for its intoxicating fragrance.

Burgundy Truffle

Harvested in early autumn, the Burgundy Truffle brings a refined, hazelnut-like aroma that pairs beautifully with simple, seasonal dishes.

Behind the Harvest

Our Story

For more than 10 years, our family-run team has specialized in sourcing and exporting fresh truffles from the forests around Veliko Tarnovo. Working alongside more than 30 experienced hunters and suppliers, we harvest exclusively local Bulgarian truffles and deliver them across Europe — to clients in Italy, France, Spain, Germany, and beyond. Our specially trained dogs work alongside our foragers, using their keen sense of smell to locate truffles hidden deep beneath the forest floor.
